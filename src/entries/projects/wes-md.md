A manga reader app, inspired by the open-source reader **Tachiyomi**. It's built in **Flutter** and fetches data directly from the **MangaDex API**.

I made it for personal use and plan to publish it on Google Play as a free app. My goal was to relearn Flutter app development and how to use **state management** properly.
