An inventory management web app for **NusaNet**, close to a full ERP. It covers:

- stock transfers between warehouses
- incoming items to warehouses
- QC of items in warehouses
- purchase orders and sales orders, and more

I built it with **2 other collaborators**, using Laravel 9, Laravel Breeze for authentication, and AdminLTE for the dashboard templates. This was part of my freelance work for Nusanet (Mar 2023 – Jan 2024).
