My final thesis. I implemented **Goal-Oriented Action Planning (GOAP)** in **Unreal Engine 4** to make AI more adaptive in a turn-based game, and compared it against an AI driven by a **Finite-State Machine (FSM)**.

## Results

| Measure | Winner |
|---|---|
| Realism of AI movement | GOAP |
| Player satisfaction | GOAP |
| Perceived difficulty | FSM, mostly because it had more resources available than the GOAP AI |
