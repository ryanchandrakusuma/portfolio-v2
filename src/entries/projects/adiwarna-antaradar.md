Volunteer work with friends while I was a student at **Universitas Kristen Petra**: the website for **Adiwarna Antaradar**. It's an exhibition of final-thesis work by Petra's Visual Communication Design students, with workshops on embroidered tote bags and paper crafts.

I built the **main page and the navbar** using Bootstrap.
